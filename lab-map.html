Interactive Urine Test Strip Map

Click any test pad on the strip image below to jump directly to its description.

Glucose Pad

Detects the presence of glucose (sugar) in urine. Normal level: Negative.

Protein Pad

Screens for elevated protein levels (proteinuria). Normal level: Negative or Trace.

Blood Pad

Detects red blood cells or hemoglobin in urine. Normal level: Negative.

Ketone Pad

Measures acetoacetic acid from fat breakdown. Normal level: Negative.

pH Pad

Measures urine acidity or alkalinity. Normal range: 5.0 to 8.0.
